## Priority 1: small-hotel guests compare rental apartments (NEW)

PriceLabs research circulated 21–22 October surveyed 1,082 travellers in the US, UK, France, Spain and Portugal. Among guests of B&Bs/inns/small hotels, **82% said they would consider a short-term rental at the same or slightly lower price**, and 48% would consider one at exactly the same price. This is **stated willingness**, NOT actual conversion/lost bookings. For a specific trip, **30% of small-hotel guests** compared an STR vs **16% of large-hotel guests**. Hotel vs STR daily price correlation was 94% in Mallorca, 84% in Barcelona, not identical rates or yield.

## Priority 2: hotels use AI to price rooms, but will not delegate (NEW)

RoomPriceGenie commissioned Sapio Research to survey 450 senior hospitality revenue decision-makers across Europe, North America and Asia Pacific, original release 20 October 2026. **53% use AI for pricing recommendations; only 10% trust it to make most pricing decisions; 77% trust it to some degree**. Another 43% lack full RMS (29% spreadsheets, 14% manual/fixed). 79% believe they maximize potential revenue, while 36% review pricing at least monthly. 47% report recruitment difficulties for revenue specialists. This is a vendor-commissioned survey, not census of the world's hotels.

Original: https://roompricegenie.com/hotel-revenue-management-2026-survey-findings/ . Crosscheck separate h2c hotel-chain AI survey (91%/13% ROI) at https://internationalinvestment.biz/en/business/8925-hotels-adopt-ai-but-struggle-to-prove-returns.html ; **do not compare 91% and 53% as directly matched populations or identical AI use cases.**
